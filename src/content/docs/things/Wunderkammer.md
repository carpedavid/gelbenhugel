---
title: Wunderkammer
description: Magical vending machines
tags:
  - adventuring-company
  - magic
---
Members in good standing can open a wunderkammer with a touch. Critical items are always in stock and relics are randomly available. By withdrawing an item from a wunderkammer, a guild member immediately incurs a debt to the guild.

Characters can find at least one of each item on the *Gear* list, along with:

| Item                                               | gp  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| Geistertrank (restores all ability damage)         | 500 |
| Rations (3 uses)                                   | 10  |
| Stärkungsmittel (a tonic that removes all fatigue) | 10  |
| Schrumpftrank (shrinking potions)                  | 5   |
|                                                    |     |
