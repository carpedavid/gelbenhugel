*The Glorious City of Gelbenhügel* is a free and open city setting based on historic Munich for [Shadowdark](https://www.thearcanelibrary.com/collections/shadowdark-rpg), [Cairn](https://cairnrpg.com/), and other rules-light fantasy games. It is a diverse, inclusive, optimistic, post-post-apocalyptic, high-fantasy world filled with nature spirits, the ruins of ages past, heroic quantities of beer and pretzels, and a mysterious, mythic underworld.

*Gelbenhügel* is free — all material developed for it is available at no cost. Additionally, the source assets of the project will always be made available for ease of reuse: markdown for words and Affinity or Procreate files for maps and images.

*Gelbenhügel* is open — it is dedicated to the public domain. ALL words, cartography, and art produced by Amalara Game Studio for the project are available under the [Creative Commons Zero (CC0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) designation, which means that you can copy, modify, and distribute any element made available here for personal or commercial use without asking for permission.
