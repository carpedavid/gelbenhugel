---
title: Questing Medallion
description: A magical medallion that allows for a quick and safe return
tags:
  - magic
  - rechargable
---
Powered by the patron spirits of the guild, the Questing Medallion is the primary benefit of membership.

As an action, a member in good standing may speak the command word and be *teleported* back to their guild house. This command will work anywhere within the borders of the world of Gilea, but does not allow the individual to be transported across planar boundaries.

Recharge the medallion by donating 100 gold to the guild's operating funds.
