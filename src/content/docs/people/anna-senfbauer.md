- Mayor of Gelbenhügel. Commonly found at the [Rathaus](../places/rathaus.md) during the day.
- Patron of [Brauhaus Weißwolf](../places/brauhaus-weisswolf.md), the smallest of the four breweries. Due to her position as mayor, her beer is now prominently on tap in the [Ratskeller](../places/ratskeller.md)
- Her non-noble mustard farming family bought the brewery when the owner died with no heirs.
- Previous family's symbol was a wolf, but they still use it on the bottle.
