**Gnomes** are the short, stout Volk of the hills. They are shorter than dwarves, but otherwise resemble humans. They are highly social, sharing childrearing and other domestic duties within large extended families.

You know the Common and Gnomish languages.

> [!info] Cairn
> - +2 Willpower
> - **Trickery**: Three times per day, you can cause a damage roll against you or an ally who you can see to be `impaired`.

> [!info] Shadowdark
