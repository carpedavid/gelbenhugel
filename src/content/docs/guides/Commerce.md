In the Age of Empires, the gold coin became the world-wide standard. Silver coins are worth one-tenth of a gold coin, and bronze coins are worth one-tenth of a silver. In Gelbenhügel, breweries also offer their own coins, each redeemable for one beer. Patrons typically buy ten coins at a time and get one free.
## Silver weapons

Cost x10 to get a silver version of a weapon. 1 GP to get a weapon silvered. Wears off after 1d4 fights. When used against creatures that aren't vulnerable to silver, 2-in-6 chance that the weapon is no longer usable.
