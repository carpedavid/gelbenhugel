- Wirtshaus — large-scale breweries that produce lagers.
- Bierküchen — small-scale beer kitchens that brew ales.
- Sprudelwasser — naturally effervescent sparkling water. The second most commonly consumed beverage after lager.
- Glühwein — A warm, spiced alcoholic beverage made by heating red or sometimes white wine with various mulling spices such as cinnamon, cloves, star anise, and citrus peel, often sweetened with sugar or honey.
- Kinderpunsch — A warm, non-alcoholic punch typically made from fruit juices and spices, popular during the winter season
