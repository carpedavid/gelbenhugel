---
title: Maßkrug
description: A magical beer stein
tags:
  - magic
  - rechargable
---
No self-respecting adventurer would venture outside a city, let alone into a dark and dangerous dungeon without their maßkrug in hand. When this stoneware beer stein is filled with exactly one liter of liquid, you can use transform that liquid, no matter how putrid, fetid, or toxic, into exactly one liter of pure, thirst-quenching beer.

Recharge by filling with actual beer, which is happily absorbed by the stein.
