**Dwarves** are the short, bearded Volk of the mountains. They have skin the color of granite and eyes as black as coal. They are all bald, so they decorate, dye, and style their beards.

> [!info] Cairn
> - +2 Strength
> - **Iron Gut**: Poison damage against you (regardless of source) is always `impaired`
