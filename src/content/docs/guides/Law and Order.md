Royal guards

Judges

Inquisitorial system. Charges can be brought by another Volk (or Volk representing another creature) or by the court itself.

Major crimes

Murder, manslaughter, arson, robbery, and rape

Murder is punishable by beheading. Manslaughter, arson, robbery are punishable by banning. Rape is punishable by immolation.

Minor crimes

Mopery — idleness or aversion to work. Punishable by imprisonment.
