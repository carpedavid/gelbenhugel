As a native of the glorious city of Gelbenhügel, there are a few things that your character would consider basic knowledge.

## Spirits are everywhere

*Gelbenhügel* and the lands that surround it are filled with spirits. They inhabit and animate mountains, lakes, rivers, trees, clouds, fields, and waterfalls. They cause the rain to fall, the rivers to flow, the wind to gust, and the crops to grow. They are the forces of nature, they are everywhere, and keeping them happy is the key to ensuring good fortune.

Because the spirits that animate the world are immanent in the daily lives of *Gelbenhügeler*, there isn’t much debate about their shape, substance, or ethos. In fact, the relationship between an average person and the spirits they interact with is primarily transactional and hyper-local.

Farmers will take care of the spirits that inhabit their fields so that the crops will grow and the harvest will be bountiful. Fishermen tend to the spirits of the streams and river. Everyone pays homage to the ancestor spirits that watch over their families; the spirits are part of the fabric of daily life.

## Magic is everywhere

Gelbenhügel is filled with magical spells, powers, items, and effects. In these lands, magic is the result of manipulating *Wesen*, the vital essence that permeates all things. Some creatures entreat spirits to manipulate Wesen on their behalf while others are able to manipulate it directly. Some creatures produce magical effects by learning to control the Wesen within themselves and others produce them by using magical weapons, armor, and other wondrous items.

Everyone in Gelbenhügel expects to see and use magic in their everyday lives. Most peasants have one or two common magical items in their possession, alchemists offer basic medical care, and hedge mages use low-level magic to provide useful services.

## Portals are everywhere

The Great War destroyed lives, cultures, and entire empires but it also left the veils between worlds in tatters. Since the war ended, the Volk of Gelbenhügel have discovered a multitude of gates and portals that connect their city to other planes, worlds, and dimensions. Some of these portals are easily discernible, while others blend into the background of the city. Some take no effort to open, while others require considerable effort.

In addition, Gelbenhügel is filled with pocket dimensions — worlds within worlds. The bottom of a forgotten well may be ten times the size it should be and contain an ancient slumbering horror, shrines to various spirits contain entire worlds, and mysterious staircases lead into impossible landscapes.

## Undead are everywhere

When a sentient being dies, its spirit typically moves from the physical world to the spirit world. The spirit's movement opens a conduit through which necrotic energy flows. Without a *Geistliche* to perform the proper rites and guide the spirit to the netherworld, the necrotic energy will reanimate the deceased creature’s corpse, creating a zombie.

Spirits of Volk that die with unfinished business often remain trapped in the physical world, haunting a person or place until they can be put to rest. Others become trapped by the intensity of their emotions — typically jealousy, rage, or hatred — and spend eternity tormenting the living.
