**Elves** are the slender, androgynous Volk of the woods. They have skin the color of tree bark, sapphire-green eyes, and flower-filled hair that resembles cotton, thistledown, or dandelion fluff.

> [!info] Cairn
> - +2 DEX
> - Ranged attacks are always `enhanced`
